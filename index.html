About us | Sobre nosotros

Our approach

A thorough clinical interview is at the beginning of our relationship with every patient. All questions are answered and possible treatment options are discussed. We then we agree on a treatment plan and start working on it.

Therapies are based on

  • manual therapies including massage,
  • detailed patient information,
  • therapeutic exercise.
We believe that, in many cases, the best physiotherapy approach is one that empowers patients to manage their condition independently in the long term. Our aim is to give you the knowledge and skills you need to address your concerns without having to rely on ongoing physiotherapy. Of course, we remain available to answer questions and help you deal with any problems that may arise later.

Areas of practice

We treat a wide range of conditions. Based on the needs of our patients, we have a particular focus on:

  • Prehabilitation: preparing for surgery, particularly joint replacement surgery,
  • Rehabilitation: regaining function after knee, hip and shoulder replacement surgery,
  • Neurological conditions: rehabilitation following a stroke and support for people living with Parkinson's disease,
  • Gait: helping people who have difficulty walking or are at increased risk of falling,
  • Respiratory physiotherapy: supporting people with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, cystic fibrosis and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease,
  • Oncology: supporting recovery after cancer treatment and helping patients maintain function during treatment.
