Stroke Rehabilitation Physiotherapy in Guardamar del Segura

Physiotherapy tailored to your situation, abilities and everyday life.

After a stroke, activities that once felt straightforward can become challenging. Walking, getting up from a chair, maintaining balance or carrying out everyday tasks may require a different approach.

At InBalance physiotherapy, based in El Raso, Guardamar del Segura, we provide physiotherapy tailored to the needs of people following a stroke. We focus on each person's individual difficulties, taking into account their current abilities, personal goals and everyday activities.

Our aim is to help you work on the abilities you retain, develop strategies for managing difficulties and maintain participation in everyday life as far as your circumstances allow.

Who can benefit from stroke rehabilitation physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy following a stroke may be helpful for people experiencing difficulties such as:

  • Have recently experienced a stroke and need to begin or continue rehabilitation.
  • Are further along in their recovery and want to continue working on their physical abilities.
  • Have difficulty walking, maintaining balance or changing position, for example when getting up from a chair or bed.
  • Have limitations in the use of an arm or leg.
  • Need to work on everyday activities that have become more difficult following a stroke.
  • Want to maintain their physical abilities and adapt their activities to their current situation.

We also work with people who have been living with the effects of a stroke for some time. Treatment is adapted to the individual's needs, regardless of how long ago the stroke occurred.

Support and information for family members concerning everyday tasks is provided.

Our approach to treatment

Every treatment programme begins with an individual assessment. We want to understand your situation, the difficulties you experience, the activities that matter to you and the goals that make sense in your everyday life.

Following the assessment, we discuss the available treatment options with you and propose an approach tailored to your needs.

What can we work on together?

Goals are established individually. Depending on your situation, treatment may focus on areas such as:

  • Walking and mobility: practising walking and addressing movement difficulties that affect getting around.
  • Balance and stability: working on balance and postural control through appropriately adapted exercises.
  • Changing position and transfers: practising activities such as standing up from a chair, sitting down or moving between a bed and a chair.
  • Arm and leg function: working on movement and function in affected limbs where appropriate.
  • Everyday activities: addressing the physical abilities needed for specific daily tasks.
  • Maintaining physical abilities: supporting continued physical activity and working on abilities that remain important to you.
  • Adapting to current difficulties: exploring ways to carry out activities more safely and in a manner suited to your abilities.

Not everyone needs to work on the same things. Goals are reviewed according to each person's progress, difficulties and priorities.

Physiotherapy at our clinic or at home

Our clinic is located in El Raso, Guardamar del Segura, Alicante, Spain.

We have treatment rooms and a therapeutic exercise area where we can work on different aspects of mobility and physical function.

For people who find it difficult to travel to the clinic, we also offer home physiotherapy, subject to availability and confirmation that the location is within our service area.

Home visits allow us to work in your familiar surroundings and address activities relevant to your own home. Availability depends on your location and individual circumstances.

If you are unsure which option would be most suitable for you or your family member, we can discuss this before arranging the first appointment.
