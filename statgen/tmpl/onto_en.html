# Physiotherapy After Cancer Treatment ## Support to regain physical function and return to everyday activities After cancer treatment, some people experience physical changes that make it difficult to return to their usual routines. Fatigue, reduced strength, mobility problems or complications such as lymphoedema can continue to affect everyday life after treatment has finished. At **InBalance Physiotherapy in El Raso, Guardamar del Segura**, we offer individualised physiotherapy for people recovering from cancer and its treatment. Our aim is to help you maintain or regain physical abilities, address difficulties that affect your daily life and work towards returning to the activities that matter to you. We also provide specific treatment for lymphoedema, including manual lymphatic drainage and complex decongestive therapy. **Contact us to discuss your situation and find out how we may be able to help.** ## Who is this service for? Our physiotherapy service is primarily intended for people who have completed cancer treatment and who: - Have noticed a reduction in strength, endurance or mobility. - Find walking, climbing stairs or performing everyday activities more difficult. - Want to gradually return to their usual level of physical activity. - Need guidance on adapting exercise to their current abilities. - Experience lymphoedema or other treatment-related physical difficulties that may benefit from physiotherapy. Every recovery is different. We take your medical circumstances, current abilities, difficulties and personal goals into account when planning treatment. ## Our approach to physiotherapy ### 1. Understanding your situation We begin with a detailed clinical interview to understand your medical history, the treatments you have received, your current difficulties and your priorities. We want to understand how your situation affects your everyday life and which aspects you would like to work on. Where necessary, we take your medical team's recommendations into account before starting or adapting treatment. ### 2. Treatment adapted to your needs Depending on your situation, treatment may include: - **Therapeutic exercise:** adapted exercises to work on strength, endurance, mobility and physical function. - **Balance and walking practice:** activities addressing difficulties with walking, stability and confidence in movement. - **Functional training:** practising everyday activities such as standing up from a chair, climbing stairs or moving around the home. - **Activity management:** strategies to distribute physical effort, plan breaks and adapt activity to your energy levels. - **Information and guidance:** practical advice on appropriate physical activity and how to continue working towards your goals outside the clinic. Treatment is adjusted to your abilities and to changes that may occur during recovery. ## Lymphoedema treatment Lymphoedema is a condition in which fluid accumulates in the tissues, potentially causing swelling, discomfort or a feeling of heaviness. It can develop after certain cancer treatments that affect the lymphatic system. At InBalance, we provide specific treatment for people with lymphoedema, including the following. ### Manual lymphatic drainage A specialised, gentle manual technique intended to support lymphatic fluid movement and help manage swelling when clinically appropriate. ### Complex decongestive therapy An individualised approach to lymphoedema management that may combine several measures according to your needs, including manual lymphatic drainage, compression, exercise and skin care. Treatment is adapted to your clinical situation, the location and characteristics of the lymphoedema, and your individual needs. The aims are to help manage swelling, improve comfort and make everyday activities easier. If you notice new or sudden swelling, particularly if it is accompanied by pain, redness or warmth, seek medical assessment before starting treatment. ## What goals can physiotherapy help you work towards? Depending on your situation, we may work towards: - Maintaining or improving strength and mobility. - Making walking and everyday activities easier. - Developing physical endurance within your individual limits. - Finding manageable ways to remain physically active. - Gradually returning to activities that are important to you. - Managing lymphoedema through an appropriate treatment plan. - Learning strategies to manage physical difficulties more effectively. Progress varies from person to person. We will discuss realistic goals with you and review your treatment according to your needs. ## Physiotherapy during recovery Recovery after cancer treatment does not follow the same path or timetable for everyone. Some people need to work on strength and mobility, while others need help managing lymphoedema or finding ways to return gradually to their usual activities. Our approach starts with your current situation and the things you need to work on.
